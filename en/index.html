Who it's for

Who it's for, and who it isn't.

A good fit if

You're a growing brand that designs and distributes its own products: website, stores, retailers.

You're launching a brand, or it's growing faster than your back office.

The more channels you have, the more you re-key.

Multi-brand store? Same flows, supplier side: catalogs to import, restocking, stock by brand.

The right time

A new collection to put online.

Opening wholesale.

A new ERP coming in.

Growth that forces you to hire for admin work.

Stock or order errors that hit your retailers.

Your tools: Shopify, Odoo or another ERP, Klaviyo, sometimes a PLM or PIM. And lots of spreadsheets in between.

Not a fit if

You just need to connect two tools: an off-the-shelf connector will do, and I'll tell you so.

You already have an IT department and an integrator.

You want to cut headcount: I remove re-keying, not jobs.
