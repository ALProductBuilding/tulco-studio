Personal data

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This page explains what data the tulcostudio.fr website collects when you visit it, why, how long it is kept and how to exercise your rights. In short: no Google audience measurement happens without your consent, and the site never asks for your name, email or any form.

Who is responsible for your data

Antoine Luizet, Tulco Studio, Biarritz (France).
Contact for any question about your data: contact@tulcostudio.fr

What is collected, and why

1. Google audience measurement (only if you accept). If you click “Accept all”, the site loads Google Tag Manager, which runs Google Analytics 4. This tells us how many people visit the site, where they come from and which sections they read, so it can be improved. Data involved: a random identifier stored in a cookie, pages viewed, traffic source, device and browser type, approximate country or city (Google Analytics 4 does not store your full IP address). Legal basis: your consent. If you decline, none of this is loaded.

2. Cookieless statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics). The site counts visits in aggregate with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookie and does not track you across sites. Legal basis: Tulco Studio's legitimate interest in knowing how much its site is visited.

3. Hosting and technical delivery. To display the pages, your browser contacts the site's host (GitHub Pages, GitHub Inc.) and the unpkg service, which provides a JavaScript library used by the page. As with any website, these providers receive your IP address and technical information (browser, time of request) and may keep them briefly in their security logs. Legal basis: legitimate interest in running the site.

4. Booking a call. The “Book 30 min” button opens a Google Calendar booking page. It only loads if you click it. The information you enter (name, email, time slot) is then processed by Google on behalf of Tulco Studio, solely to organise the call. It is kept for the duration of the relationship, then for at most 3 years after the last contact. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract.

The LinkedIn link is a plain link: nothing is loaded from LinkedIn unless you click it.

How long data is kept

  • Google Analytics 4 data: 14 months at most, then deleted automatically.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics statistics: aggregated, visitors are not identified.
  • Bookings and exchanges: for the duration of the relationship, then at most 3 years after the last contact.

Who can access it

Antoine Luizet, and the technical providers listed above, each for its own service: Google (Tag Manager, Analytics, Calendar), Cloudflare, GitHub and unpkg. No data is sold or shared for other purposes.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, to which Google, Cloudflare and GitHub (Microsoft) are certified, or otherwise on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask to access your data, correct or delete it, restrict or object to its use, or receive it in a readable format. Where processing relies on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting what was done before.

To do so, write to contact@tulcostudio.fr. You will get an answer within one month at most.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority: cnil.fr/fr/plaintes.

Changes

This page may change, for example if a new tool is added to the site. The last update date is shown at the top.
